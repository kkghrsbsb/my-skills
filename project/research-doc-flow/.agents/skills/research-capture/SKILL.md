# Research Capture

Read [the shared protocol](../../research-flow/protocol.md). Confirm the research question, source boundary, and desired depth before reading. State `capture`, whether sources are already available or require authorized collection, and where the result will return.

For each source actually read, record identity, date, role, stable path or link, and a useful locator. Capture only claims relevant to the question, with enough surrounding context to prevent misleading extraction. Paraphrase by default; quote sparingly when precise wording matters. Separate source content from your interpretation.

Assess the source's authority, scope, freshness, methods, incentives, and access gaps in proportion to the claim. When a claim cannot be located, verified, or compared, record that limitation rather than upgrading it to a fact. Do not create fabricated citations from search snippets, memory, or an uninspected link.

Use [the source card template](../../research-flow/templates/source.md) when a durable artifact helps. A branch session returns source cards, negative findings, and unresolved questions to the coordinator; it does not update shared research state or silently turn collection into final synthesis.
