# Research Resume

Read [the shared protocol](../../research-flow/protocol.md). Begin with read-only recovery. State whether the research is resumed or transferred, and inspect the relevant understanding file, source cards, and current artifacts.

Reconstruct the current question and its changes, sources actually captured, important data and experiment derivation links, open claims, the researcher's last restatements and confidence, and what they still need to understand. Verify central saved conclusions against source locators, input/output artifacts, and current draft text; mark inaccessible, changed, incomplete, or uncertain material clearly. Do not infer current understanding from old notes or a complete bibliography.

When the user asks to review, says they have been away for a while, or prioritizes rebuilding understanding, invite `recall` before a full explanation. Ask a few short questions grounded in the saved checkpoints and actual materials. Adapt from basic terms to causal links, boundaries, dependencies, and counterfactuals as the user's answers allow. Let the user shorten or skip the review. Correct with inspectable evidence and record only their actual restatement, self-reported confidence, and remaining gaps.

Return a recovery brief that distinguishes verified evidence, unverified notes, current decisions, derivation gaps, the researcher's expressed understanding, and unresolved questions. Preserve partial notes and existing user materials. Update `.agents/research-flow/understanding.md` only when continuation or persistence is already in scope and ownership of the shared file is clear.
