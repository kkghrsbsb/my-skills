# Research Resume

Read [the shared protocol](../../research-flow/protocol.md). Begin with `orient`-style read-only recovery. State whether the thread is resumed or transferred, and inspect the relevant state file, source cards, final artifacts, and available task reports.

Reconstruct the current question, intended deliverable, sources actually captured, important data and experiment derivation links, open claims, cognitive debt, and the next unblocked step. Verify central saved conclusions against source locators, input/output artifacts, and current draft text; mark inaccessible, changed, incomplete, or uncertain material clearly. Do not assume an old bibliography means every cited source was read or that a paused branch completed its assigned reading.

When the user asks to review, says they have been away for a while, or prioritizes rebuilding their understanding, offer `recall` before a full explanation. Ask three to seven short questions grounded in the saved cognitive checkpoints and actual materials. Adapt their depth from basic terms and objects to causal links, boundaries, dependencies, and counterfactuals. Let the user skip or shorten the review. After answers, correct only with inspectable evidence, explain the smallest missing link, and record specific new or resolved cognitive debt rather than a quiz transcript.

Return a recovery brief that distinguishes verified evidence, unverified notes, current decisions, derivation gaps, cognitive debt, and unresolved work. Preserve partial notes and existing user materials. Update `.agents/research-flow/state.md` only when continuation or persistence is already in scope and coordination ownership is clear.
